# 滤镜缩略图素材

当前素材为用户选定的海滩图，使用内置 image_gen 工具生成。已复制到 `LumaCamera/Resources/Assets.xcassets/FilterLandscape.imageset/landscape.png`。蓝天、白云、海水、沙滩和右侧绿树提供自然颜色及中性色参照；所有滤镜与原片共享同一张图，拍摄及相册编辑共用。

## 最终提示词

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square photograph for camera filter preview thumbnails. A simple beautiful seaside landscape photographed in natural daylight, with blue sky and a few broad soft white clouds across the top third, clear blue-turquoise ocean across the middle, a gently curving pale golden sand beach in the lower left foreground, and a coherent patch of lush green coastal trees on the right side. Open sea horizon, small gentle white-edged waves. A believable single coastal scene with broad readable color areas: blue, turquoise, white, warm sand, leafy green. Keep trees on right clearly inside the composition, not only at the extreme edge, so they remain visible in a circular thumbnail crop. Clean uncluttered composition, realistic balanced color, soft detail, no dramatic color grade, no oversaturation, no sunset. No desert dunes, lake, mountains, buildings, people, boats, umbrellas, text, logos, borders or watermark.

沙漠湖泊方案仅作为对比预览，未用于 App。此次没有修改滤镜调色交互；用户录像中的二维调色板仅确认可实现，后续另行安排。
